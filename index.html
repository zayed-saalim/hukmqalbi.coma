About the Founder

Saalim Zayed founded Hukm Qalbi as an answer to a simple question: what would it look like to build things that actually mattered — and to build them properly?

His work sits at the intersection of entrepreneurship and craft. He writes, designs, builds digital products, and leads brand and media projects through Hukm Qalbi — with a consistent focus on purpose, clarity and long-term value.

He is particularly interested in how ideas move from thought to form: how a sentence becomes a brand, how a brand becomes a product, and how a product becomes something people genuinely rely on.
